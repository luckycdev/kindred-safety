1. What Kindred is

Kindred is a consumer application that lets a subscriber broadcast live video and location to a private group of people they have personally invited, and to raise an alert to that group. It is a communication tool.

2. What Kindred is not

Kindred is not an emergency service, a monitored alarm, a medical alert system, or a substitute for contacting your local emergency number. We do not dispatch help, we do not monitor streams, and no one at the company is watching for an alert on your behalf. If you are in danger, call the emergency number for your country.

3. Eligibility and accounts

You must be 16 or older to hold a subscription, and 13 or older to be a member of someone else's circle with a guardian's consent. You are responsible for keeping your credentials and your archive passphrase secure. The passphrase cannot be recovered; if it is lost, your archive is permanently unreadable.

4. Acceptable use

  • Do not use Kindred to record people in places where they have a reasonable expectation of privacy.
  • Do not use Kindred to monitor another adult without their ongoing, informed consent.
  • Do not use Kindred to harass, stalk, intimidate or defame anyone.
  • Do not attempt to give a viewer seat the ability to start a camera. This is a hard product boundary.
  • Follow the recording and consent laws that apply where you are. They vary a lot, and they're your responsibility.

We may suspend an account that we reasonably believe is being used in these ways, and will say why.

We aim for high availability but do not guarantee it. Streaming depends on your device, your battery, your mobile network and third-party relay infrastructure — any of which can fail at the worst possible moment. Build your safety plan on the assumption that this app might not work.
