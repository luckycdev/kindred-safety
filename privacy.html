Privacy policy

Sample text · last updated 1 March 2026

Placeholder text in a demo project. This site has no backend and collects nothing whatsoever — not even analytics. The policy below describes what the fictional product would do.

The short version

  • Clips are encrypted on your device with a key derived from your passphrase. We can't read them.
  • Location is shared only during a stream, or with the circle you chose, at the frequency you set.
  • No advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, no data brokers, no model training on your content.
  • Deleting means deleting — from live storage and from backups within 72 hours.

What we collect

Account data

Name, email address, mobile number, plan, and the identifiers your app store gives us for billing. We keep this for as long as the account exists, plus 90 days.

Location data

Coordinates, accuracy, speed and heading, captured every three seconds during a stream and every fifteen minutes when idle location sharing is enabled. Trails older than 30 days are deleted automatically. You can turn idle sharing off entirely; SOS location is captured only at the moment you trigger it.

Media

Video and audio you broadcast, stored encrypted with a key we do not hold, retained for 90 days by default.

Diagnostics

Crash traces, connection timings and error codes. These contain no video, no audio and no coordinates, and are only sent if you opt in.

What we do not collect

Contacts, photo library, browsing activity, advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or any information about people who are not Kindred users. Circle invites are matched by a salted hash of the phone number so we never store the numbers of people who decline.

Who we share with

The members of your circle, and nobody else, except: our payment processor (billing identifiers only), our relay provider (encrypted stream packets only), and our SMS provider (phone number and a fixed alert template, used only for SOS fallback). We publish the list of these providers and update it 30 days before adding one.

Legal requests

We respond to valid legal process, and we tell you when we do unless we're legally barred from it. Because clips are encrypted with a key we don't hold, what we can produce is limited to account and billing metadata. We publish an aggregate transparency report twice a year.

Your rights

Export, correct or delete your data at any time from Settings → Privacy, or by emailing privacy@kindred.example. Exports are machine-readable and include your location history, clip index and account record. Deletion requests complete within 72 hours.

Children

Kindred is not designed for children under 13. A guardian may add a 13–15 year old to a circle, and that member can see exactly what is shared about them, on the same screen the guardian sees.

Contact

privacy@kindred.example — a real person answers within a week, and within 30 days for formal requests.
