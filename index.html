Why Kindred

Six things that happen
the second you press the button.

No dialling. No explaining where you are. No waiting for someone to pick up.

Live video, instantly

Front or rear camera streams to your circle in about a second and a half. They see the scene as it unfolds, not a recording afterwards.

Live location trail

A breadcrumb map updates every few seconds, so your circle knows not just where you are but which way you're heading.

One-tap SOS

Push, hold, release. Every member of your circle gets a push notification, an SMS fallback and a direct link into the stream.

Panic siren

A 110 dB siren plus a strobing screen. Draw attention, buy time, and keep the stream running in the background while it sounds.

Cloud clips you own

Everything you stream is stored encrypted, timestamped and geotagged. Download the original file any time, or delete it forever.

A circle, not an audience

You pick who can watch — up to nine people. No public feed, no discovery tab, no strangers. Ever.

Inside the app

Four screens. That's the entire product.

We deliberately kept it small. Under stress, nobody reads a menu.

The stream is the interface

Big SOS button, camera flip, mic mute, siren. Everything else is hidden until you need it. The stream keeps running if you pocket the phone, lock the screen, or lose signal for up to 40 seconds.

  • Adaptive bitrate down to 240p on weak signal
  • Buffers offline and uploads when signal returns
  • Screen can be blacked out while still recording

Where they are, which way they're going

Location updates every three seconds during a stream and every fifteen minutes otherwise. Your circle sees a trail, a speed, and a best-guess address — not just a pin.

  • Breadcrumb trail with timestamps
  • Safe-place arrival and departure pings
  • Shareable one-time link for non-members

Nine people, maximum

Every plan is sized by how many people stream and how many people watch. Roles are explicit: a viewer can never start your camera, and you can revoke anyone in two taps.

  • Separate streamer and viewer seats
  • Per-member notification rules
  • Instant revoke, with an audit entry

Your footage, your call

Clips are encrypted at rest with a key derived from your passphrase. Export the untouched original with its metadata sidecar, or delete it — deletion is real and permanent.

  • Original file + SHA-256 checksum
  • GPS, heading and device metadata sidecar
  • 90-day retention, or delete on demand

Questions

The things people ask first.

Can Kindred see my streams?

No. Clips are encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase before they leave the device, and we don't hold that key. If you lose your passphrase, your archive is unrecoverable — that's the trade we chose.

Does it work if my phone is locked?

Yes. The SOS trigger is available from the lock screen widget, the side-button shortcut, or a paired Bluetooth remote. The screen can stay black while the stream runs.

What happens when I lose signal?

The stream buffers locally for up to 40 seconds and drops the bitrate before it drops the connection. Anything it couldn't send live uploads automatically once you're back on a network.

Is this a replacement for calling emergency services?

No, and we'd never claim otherwise. Kindred alerts the people who already care about you. Call your local emergency number when you need professional help.

How much battery does a stream use?

Around 12% per hour at 720p on a recent phone, or about 7% per hour in low-power 480p mode. Idle location sharing costs roughly 1% per day.

Can I use it in another country?

Streaming works anywhere you have data. SMS fallback alerts depend on local carrier support and are billed to us, not to you.
