What counts as a stream hour?

Wall-clock time with your camera live. A 90-second SOS burst costs 90 seconds. Idle location sharing costs nothing.

What happens if I run out of hours mid-stream?

Nothing, immediately — we never cut a live broadcast. You'll go into overage at $0.90 per hour, billed the following month, and we'll tell you the moment it starts.

Can I change plan size later?

Any time, both directions. Upgrades apply instantly and are prorated; downgrades take effect at your next renewal so nobody loses access mid-cycle.

Do viewers need their own subscription?

No. Viewer seats come out of the plan of whoever is streaming. Your circle installs the app, accepts the invite, and pays nothing.
