Written walkthrough

The same setup, if you'd rather read than watch.

01

Install & verify

Download from the App Store or Google Play, verify your phone number, then set a passphrase. Write it down somewhere physical — we can't reset it, and it's the key to your clip archive.

02

Grant three permissions

Camera, microphone and “always” location. The app explains what each one is used for before it asks, and it works — degraded — if you decline location.

03

Invite your circle

Send invites by phone number. Each person picks streamer or viewer. You can change either role later without re-inviting.

04

Bind the trigger

Add the lock-screen widget, then enable the side-button shortcut in Settings → Trigger. Test it once — the app has a rehearsal mode that alerts nobody.

05

Mark your safe places

Home, work, a campus, a friend's flat. Arrivals and departures generate a quiet ping instead of an alert.

06

Do a dry run together

Rehearsal mode streams to your circle with a clear “this is a test” banner. Do it once so nobody's first experience of the alert is a real one.

Troubleshooting

The stream starts but my circle sees a black screen

Almost always a camera permission that was granted for “this time only”. Open Settings → Permissions → Camera and switch it to “while using the app”, then restart the broadcast.

SOS doesn't fire from the lock screen

iOS: Settings → Accessibility → Side Button → Triple-press → Kindred. Android: Settings → Gestures → Quick Launch. Battery optimisation can also suspend the listener — exclude Kindred from it.

Location shows the wrong street

Indoors, the first fix comes from Wi-Fi triangulation and can be 100 m out. It converges within about 20 seconds outdoors. The accuracy radius in the viewer console is always honest about this.

A clip won't decrypt

The passphrase is wrong, or it was changed after the clip was recorded. Old clips keep their old key — try the passphrase you used at the time of recording.
